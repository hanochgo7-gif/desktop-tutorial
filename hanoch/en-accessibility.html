Accessibility statement

I want everyone to be able to use this site comfortably, including people with disabilities. The site follows WCAG 2.2 at level AA and the Israeli standard IS 5568, and I keep improving it.

What the site does

  • Pages have a clear heading structure, and every meaningful image has a text description.
  • The whole site works with a keyboard, and keyboard focus is clearly marked.
  • A "Skip to services" link at the top of the page jumps straight to the content.
  • All text is at least 13px, and every button and link on phones is at least 44px tall.
  • The contact form works fully with a keyboard and tells screen readers which step you are on.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, you get a quiet version of the site: no animation, no 3D and no smooth scrolling.
  • Windows that open, such as the project stories, close with Esc, and focus returns to where it was.
  • Messages that change on the page are announced to screen readers.

The accessibility menu

A button in the corner of the screen opens a menu with these options: larger text, high contrast, highlighted links, a readable font, stop animations, a large cursor and stronger keyboard focus. Your choices are saved in your browser only.

What is not perfect yet

The opening animation (the hands and the glass ח) is decorative: everything it says also appears as plain text. The project videos are silent screen recordings shown for illustration. If any part is hard to use, I want to hear about it and fix it.
