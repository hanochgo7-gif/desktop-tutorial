Privacy policy

hgpro.io belongs to HG Studio, the business of Hanoch Gutovski. This page explains what information is collected when you browse the site or contact me, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and what your rights are.

In short

  • There is no sign-up on the site. Your details are kept only if you send a form on the site with a phone number or email, or contact me on WhatsApp, by email or by phone.
  • Google Analytics and Google Ads load only after you accept cookies. Until then the site sends nothing to Google. You can withdraw your consent at any time.
  • The Meta Pixel is not active on the site, and no browsing information is sent to Meta.
  • Questions you type to the digital advisor are sent to Anthropic to write an answer. I do not store the conversations.
  • I do not sell personal information, and I do not send marketing messages without your explicit prior consent.

Who is responsible for the information

Hanoch Gutovski, HG Studio. For any privacy question, write to boss@hgpro.io, on WhatsApp or by phone at +972 54-552-2053.

Information you give me

When you contact me on WhatsApp, by email or by phone, I receive what you chose to send: your name, phone number or email, and the content of your message. When you send a form on the site, the details are saved in my inquiries system, as explained in the next section. The brief form on the English home page works differently: it sends nothing to a server, and keeps a draft only in your browser until you choose to send it on WhatsApp or by email.

You are not legally required to share these details, but without a way to reach you I cannot get back to you. I use the information to answer you, prepare a proposal, provide the service you ordered, issue receipts and invoices, and meet my legal obligations.

My inquiries system

The forms on the site send your details directly from your browser to my inquiries management system (HG OS):

  • The brief form on the site (the Hebrew brief page): name, phone or email (or both), business name if you gave one, what you need, when you want to start, and anything you wrote in the free-text field.
  • A call-back request: name, phone, the day and time you picked, and anything you wrote in the "what to talk about" field.

An inquiry is saved only if it has a phone number or an email. The address of the page the form was sent from is saved with it. If you accepted cookies, the source that brought you is saved too: campaign parameters (UTM), an ad click ID (for example gclid or fbclid), the landing page and the referring site. The site keeps this source in your browser for up to 90 days, and sends it only together with an inquiry. Without consent, only the details you typed and the page address are saved, without its parameters.

The system is hosted by Supabase on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Only I work with this information, and I use it to get back to you about your inquiry and manage our business contact. After sending the brief you can also continue on WhatsApp, with a ready message that you decide whether to send.

When you refer someone else's business

In the referral programme on the Hebrew site you send me the name and phone number of another business owner on WhatsApp (there is no form for this on the site). Only share the details of someone who knows about it and agrees that I contact them. I use the details only to contact them about the referral, I tell them who gave me their details, and if they are not interested, I delete them.

The digital advisor (Compass)

The digital advisor is software, not a person. When you pick ready-made answers (like your type of business), nothing leaves your browser. When you type a question, the messages in the conversation go through the site's server at Vercel to Anthropic, the US company that runs the Claude AI model, to write an answer. To make the answer fit you, the site may also send the advisor the page you are on and the answers you chose in the brief or the calculator on the site (for example your type of business and what you need).

I do not store the conversations on my servers and I do not see their content. The conversation is kept only in your browser tab, and deleted when you close it. Anthropic processes the messages under its commercial terms. According to what it publishes, it deletes them within 30 days, keeps for up to two years only messages flagged as violating its usage policy, and does not train its models on them. The content of the conversation is not sent to Google.

Please don't write sensitive information to the advisor, such as card details, ID numbers, medical information or passwords.

Nothing from the chat reaches me unless you tap "Send to Hanoch on WhatsApp". Only then does a message open with the brief you approved, and you decide whether to send it.

Analytics (Google Analytics)

The site uses Google Analytics 4. Google's code loads only after you accept cookies in the notice shown on the site. Before that, and if you choose no cookies, the site sends no request at all to Google.

After you accept, Google Analytics collects information about how the site is used: which pages were viewed and when, approximate location (country and city), device and browser type, where you came from, and actions on the site such as tapping WhatsApp, phone or email, opening the call-back window, playing videos, which parts of a page were shown, which FAQ answers were opened, which steps of the brief form were filled in, sending a form, sending a message to the digital advisor, and visiting client sites. The details you typed in forms and the content of your messages are not sent to Google.

According to Google, Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses. The purpose is to understand which content helps visitors and to improve the site. The data is processed by Google, including in the United States, and kept for at most 14 months under the retention settings.

You can withdraw consent at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page. The site then deletes Google's cookies from your browser, together with the source information and the ad code it kept after you accepted.

Ad measurement (Google Ads)

When I advertise on Google, I need to know which ads brought inquiries. So, after you accept cookies, Google Ads records whether you came from an ad and whether you contacted me (for example by tapping WhatsApp), and Google Ads cookies are stored. Without consent, Google receives no information from the site. I don't use this information for personalised ads or remarketing.

The Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram)

The site has ready code for the Meta Pixel, but it is switched off: it does not load, and no browsing information from the site is sent to Meta. If I decide to turn it on, I will update this policy first, and it will load only after you agree.

The ad code in the WhatsApp message

If you arrived from an ad link, the WhatsApp message that opens ends with a short code (for example "Code: M12") so I know which ad brought the inquiry. You see the code before sending and can delete it. Without cookie consent, when you move to another page on the site the code travels in that page's address, without a cookie and without being stored in your browser. If you accepted cookies, it is kept in your browser until you close the tab, and does not travel in the address.

WhatsApp, email and phone

A WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp, by Meta, with a ready message. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp, and you can change or delete the text before that. A message you send is also subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy. When you arrive from a Google ad, the link sometimes passes first through a transfer page on the site (hgpro.io/go/wa), and the text of the ready message then also appears in the address the site's server receives. An email you send passes through your email provider and mine. Links to client sites lead to sites with their own privacy policies.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel Inc., a US company, and served through a worldwide network of servers. Like any web server, Vercel processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time, to show you the site and protect it. The digital advisor's server also runs at Vercel, and remembers your IP address for a short time only to prevent abuse. This data is kept for a short time under Vercel's settings, and I do not use it to identify visitors. Fonts, code libraries, images and videos load from the site's own server, not from other companies' servers.

Who the information is shared with

I do not sell or rent personal information. Information is passed only to the providers named on this page (Supabase, Vercel, Anthropic and Google), as far as their service requires, to my accountant and the tax authorities for clients who received a service, and to anyone the law requires me to disclose it to.

Transfers outside Israel

The providers I work with are outside Israel:

  • Supabase: my inquiries system, on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt).
  • Vercel: site hosting and the digital advisor's server, a US company with servers worldwide.
  • Anthropic: writing the digital advisor's answers, in the United States.
  • Google: analytics and ad measurement, only after cookie consent, in the United States and other countries.

Information is transferred to them under their own data protection terms. A message you choose to send on WhatsApp passes through Meta.

How long information is kept

  • An inquiry that did not become a project: deleted 24 months after our last contact.
  • Clients: kept during the work and for 7 years after it, as required by tax and accounting law.
  • Conversations with the digital advisor: not stored by me. At Anthropic, as explained above.
  • Google Analytics data: at most 14 months.
  • The source that brought you, in your browser: up to 90 days.
  • Technical data at Vercel: a short time.

You can ask me to delete your details sooner, as explained under "Your rights".

Security

The site is served only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Details you leave in a form are saved in my inquiries system, which is protected by access permissions and an encrypted connection, and only I work with that information. No system is completely secure, but I take reasonable measures to protect the information.

Your rights

  • To see the information I hold about you (section 13 of the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law).
  • To ask me to correct or delete information that is not accurate, complete, clear or up to date (section 14).
  • To ask me to delete your details. I will delete them, unless the law requires me to keep them (for example invoices).
  • To withdraw cookie consent at any time, with the "Cookie settings" link.
  • If you are in the European Union, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also have rights under the GDPR: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to complain to your local supervisory authority.

To use a right, write to me at boss@hgpro.io, on WhatsApp or by phone at +972 54-552-2053. I will answer within 30 days, and if needed I will ask for a detail that confirms the request came from you. You can also complain to the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority at the Ministry of Justice.

Legal basis (for visitors from the EU)

Analytics and ad measurement: your consent. Answering an inquiry, the digital advisor and preparing a proposal: steps you asked for before a contract, and a legitimate interest in answering inquiries. Keeping client records: a legal obligation.

Marketing messages

I do not send marketing messages by email, SMS or WhatsApp without your explicit prior consent, as required by section 30A of the Israeli Communications Law. The forms on the site do not ask for that consent, so I will contact you only about your inquiry. If I ever ask for consent to marketing, it will be separate, and every such message will include a simple way to opt out.

Children

The site is meant for businesses and business owners, and is not intended for children or teenagers under 18.
