Questions people ask before we start.

Didn’t find your answer? Write to me, or ask the digital advisor in the corner of the screen.

Who actually builds the site?

I do, Hanoch Gotovski. HG Studio is a one-person studio: I design, write the code and produce the videos myself, and you talk to me directly from the first call until after the site is live.

What happens after I send a message?

It comes straight to me, and I answer it myself, not a bot or a call center. We set up a short intro call, and then you get a written proposal with a fixed price and a timeline. No commitment.

What does the "Made with AI" label mean?

Some of the images and videos on this site were made with AI, and they are labeled. The screens of client work are real and shown as they are.

Why are there no prices on the site?

Because every business needs something different. After a short call you get a written proposal with a fixed price and a timeline, and what the proposal says is what you pay. No surprises.

How long until the site is live?

A landing page (Presence) goes live within a week at most. A full site (Business or Signature) goes live within two weeks at most, from our call to launch.

Who owns the site in the end?

You do. The site, the code, the domain and the content all belong to you.

My site is on Wix or WordPress. Will I lose my place on Google?

You shouldn’t. The content moves over, and every old address Google knows is redirected to the matching page on the new site. The move is included in the Business package.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. The Business and Signature packages include a content system where you update text, images and prices yourself.

Do I have to write all the text myself?

No. You can bring existing text, or I can write it in your business’s voice. Marketing copywriting is an add-on that works with any package.

What happens after launch?

Support is included: one month with Presence and Business, three months with Signature. After that you can continue with the Quiet plan (hosting, backups, monitoring and up to an hour of changes a month) or the Growth plan (up to four hours a month, a monthly report and SEO improvements).

Will the site be accessible?

Yes. Accessibility adjustments and an accessibility menu are included in every package.
