Questions.What people ask me before we start

Didn't find your answer? Send me a message and I'll get back to you.

How long does it really take?

Two weeks at most, from the intro call to a live site. A landing page is usually ready within a week. To keep to schedule I need your content and images at the start, and if you don't have them, I'll help you prepare them.

Why not build it myself on Wix?

On Wix you get a template thousands of other businesses are using. With me, the site is designed around your business, loads faster, is ready for Google from day one, and is built to bring in leads. And you don't spend your evenings on it.

Who writes the content and provides the images?

You know the business best, so you bring the raw material: what you do, for whom, and what makes you different. I organize it, write it and design it. If there are no photos, we can produce images and video with AI, or add full copywriting.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. From the Business package up there's a simple content manager where you change text, images and prices without touching code. After launch I'll show you how, in a short call.

What about the domain and hosting?

I take care of all of it: we register the domain in your name, connect fast and secure hosting, and set up business email if you need it. The domain and the site are registered to you, and stay yours.

Will the site show up on Google?

Every site is built on the right foundations for Google: speed, titles, descriptions, business markup and a sitemap. That way Google understands what you do and where. No one can promise first place, but we can build the strongest base, and keep improving it with ongoing SEO.

Is the price here the final price?

The prices here are a starting point. After the intro call you get a detailed written proposal, and what's in it is what you pay. I'm a VAT-exempt business, so there's no VAT on top. If you want to add something along the way, we agree on it first.

What happens after the site goes live?

For the first month I'm with you at no extra cost: small fixes, questions and guidance. After that you can continue with a monthly care plan, or carry on yourself. Either way, the site is yours.

Will the site be accessible?

Every site is built accessible from the start: contrast, keyboard navigation, alt text for images and an accessibility menu. If your business is legally required to publish an accessibility statement, we run a proper audit before launch.

Don't 3D and animation slow a site down?

Not if they're built right. The 3D loads only when it's needed, quality adapts to the device, and on a weak phone the site switches to a lighter version. This site is built the same way.
