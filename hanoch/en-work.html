Ani Gibor.
Your child, in the lead role.

This isn’t a brochure site. It’s a complete product I designed and built from scratch: parents pick one of ten worlds and send one photo from their phone, and their child becomes a 3D animated character who looks just like them. The film comes with their name, their age and a message from the parents, ready to screen at the party. The site, ordering, payment and the film production line all work together, built by one pair of hands.

  • One photo becomes a character who stays the same through the whole film
  • 10 worlds, each with a narrated trailer and music
  • Ready in 5 business days, or in 48 hours with express delivery

TV-grade commercials.
Not a single shoot day.

Three commercials I produced from scratch with AI: script, consistent characters, cinematography, an edit cut to the music and voiceover. Elef is a cinematic spot for olive oil from the Galilee, from the harvest to a drop on warm bread. HG is a 20-second fragrance spot: a man, a rainy night in the city, and a scent that stays in the room after he leaves. Scent Check is a 12-second vertical reel for social: three HG looks, and you pick one. Below them, two ads for HG Studio itself: vertical, for Reels, with real screens from client sites.

  • Script, shots, edit and sound in one pair of hands
  • No actors, no location and no crew
  • Short cuts for social: 15 and 6 seconds

Made entirely with AI, with no real filming.
