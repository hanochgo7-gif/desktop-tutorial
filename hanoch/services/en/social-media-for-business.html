Echo: your business on Instagram and Facebook

Many customers meet a business on social media first, and only then on its website. Echo sets up tidy Instagram and Facebook pages, fills them with content that stops the scroll, and brings interested people all the way to WhatsApp. From the same hands that build the websites and make the commercials.

Get a quote on WhatsApp

What we do

  • Audit and plan: how your pages look today, what competitors do, and what your customers look for.
  • Setup: bio, WhatsApp and call buttons, highlights with covers, and design templates in your brand language.
  • Content every week: Reels, posts, carousels and Stories, with captions in English and Hebrew as needed.
  • AI commercials: short films with a story, characters and voice-over, without a film crew.
  • Shoot days at your business: real photos and video of the place, the people and the products.
  • Meta paid ads: audiences, ads, budget and tracking of every lead.
  • Replies: comments, messages, and an auto-reply that sends leads to WhatsApp.

Packages

Launch: your pages set up or rebuilt, a design kit, the first nine posts and one AI commercial. Steady: content every week, an AI commercial every month, publishing, replies and a monthly report. Full: all of that, plus paid ads, a shoot day at your business, two AI commercials a month and a landing page for every campaign. See the packages.

What is different

Whoever builds your website already knows the business, so nothing needs explaining twice. The commercials are made with AI at a cinematic level, so a new film every month needs no shoot day. And at the end of each month we look at leads, messages and WhatsApp clicks, not just likes.

Good to know

The pages, accounts and content belong to you. Nothing goes live before you approve it. The paid ad budget is paid directly to the platform, separately. No one can promise a number of followers, sales or leads, but everything can be measured and improved every month.

FAQ

Do I need to be on camera?

No. Content can be built from AI commercials, photos of the place and the products, and text. People who do appear tend to build trust faster.

When will I see results?

No one can promise a timeline. The first month goes into setup and learning, and from there we measure every month and change what does not work.

What about the paid ad budget?

You set it with us, and it is paid directly to Meta, separately from the service. You can start small and grow only what brings leads.

Who approves the content?

You do. At the start of each month you get a content plan, and nothing goes live before you approve it.
