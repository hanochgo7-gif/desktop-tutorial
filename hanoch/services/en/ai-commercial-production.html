AI commercial production

A TV-grade commercial used to need a crew, actors, a location and a shoot day. Today a cinematic film can be produced with AI in days, for a fraction of the cost. HGPRO writes the script, creates consistent characters, shoots every frame, cuts to the music and records the voiceover.

What you get

  • A short script and story that fits the brand.
  • Consistent characters, product and vehicles across the film.
  • Cinematic 1080p shots, an edit cut to the music, a color grade and widescreen framing.
  • Natural voiceover and legally licensed music.
  • 30, 15 and 6 second versions for TV, YouTube and social.

Examples

The archive holds two commercials made this way: HG · Unforgettable, a fragrance spot with models, a luxury car and a cliff road at night, and Before the City Wakes, a one-minute action film about a bakery and a courier in Tel Aviv. Watch in the archive.
