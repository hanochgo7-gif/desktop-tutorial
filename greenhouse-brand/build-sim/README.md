# "How a net house goes up": scroll-driven 3D illustration

Prototype for the future site (not the live site). Three.js r128 from cdnjs, everything else inline.

Ten scroll chapters: plan → marking out → galvanized poles → cables → net spread row by row → side nets and door → irrigation and planting → hail protection (storm sky, hail bouncing off the net) → net folded in winter → turnkey call to action (office 04-9854404).

## Rules kept
- Labelled "הדמיה" / "Illustration" on screen; the footer says sizes, spacing and height are not a technical specification.
- Steel is drawn light silver (galvanized) only.
- Texts come only from approved sources: the Turnkey stages (questionnaire), the "From the field" section, and the projects in the questionnaire (Western Galilee 11% net against wind and hail; Golan apples, net spread in summer and folded in winter). No dimensions or invented numbers.
