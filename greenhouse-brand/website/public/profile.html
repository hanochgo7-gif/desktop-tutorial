MIFTAH Projects

Where agriculture feels at home

MIFTAH Projects is a family company in Kfar Masaryk, Israel. We design, manufacture in our own plant, install and maintain galvanized-steel net houses for orchards and bananas, in Israel and abroad, and build turnkey farms abroad together with agricultural partners.

20+years designing and building agricultural structures
410,000 m²(41 ha) of net houses built abroad by 2015, in Mexico, India, Honduras, Spain and Georgia
Own plantpoles, nets, cables and fittings are made and checked before they ship
From 5 haproject size in Israel, and from 30 ha abroad

What we build

  • Banana net houses: 11% shade net: light and very airy.
  • Orchard net houses: 10% to 30% shade, chosen for the crop.
  • Loading sheds and windbreaks: Shade sheds for loading, unloading and handling produce. Wide spans leave room for trucks and carts to turn.
  • Maintenance, dismantling and reuse: Care for structures as nets, cables and fittings weather over the years.

How a project runs

  1. Needs mapping on site
  2. Engineering design
  3. Manufacturing in our plant
  4. Installation and maintenance

Turnkey farms abroad (with partners)

Soil testing and crop fit, business plan and budget, farm design, structures and net houses, irrigation systems, procurement and logistics, planting, local team, growing and agronomy, maintenance, harvest and marketing.
