How a project runs

  1. 01

    Site visit and needs assessment

    We meet you on the land and map the crop, soil, drainage, access and wind.

  2. 02

    Engineering design

    We calculate the loads and prepare the drawings and the bill of quantities: the list of materials the quote is based on.

  3. 03

    Manufacturing in our plant

    After the quote is approved, poles, nets, cables and fittings are made and checked in our plant.

  4. 04

    Installation and maintenance

    Our crews, trained for work at height, build the structure and maintain it over the years.

Questions

Which net is used over bananas?

An 11% shade net: light and airy, over wide spans.

How large can one banana net house be?

One structure can reach 10 ha (100 dunam), thanks to large spans between the poles.

How is a banana net house linked to grants?

In the Ministry of Agriculture's 2023 development plan, grants for irrigation and trellising in bananas are approved only when the planting is under an existing or new net house. Check the current procedure before you apply.

Does the net save water?

Yes. In the hot season it reduces evaporation, so the plantation needs less water.
