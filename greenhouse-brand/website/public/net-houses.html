The parts of the structure

  1. 1
    Net10–30% shade for orchards: against hail and weather damage at every stage of growth. 11% shade for bananas: a light, airy net against wind and weather damage.
  2. 2
    Steel cablesTensioned across the pole heads to carry the net.
  3. 3
    Galvanized steel polesMade to the design and worked in our plant, then set in rows at the span and height set in the design.
  4. 4
    Perimeter anchoringEdge cables tie the structure to the ground against wind.
  5. 5
    Trellising and supportBuilt with the structure to suit the crop: pergola or T-support trellising for kiwi, trellising in orchards, fruit walls, support for bananas.
  6. 6
    FoldingIn orchards the net can fold away in winter and spread again in spring, by hand or automatically.

Designed for the site and the client

Before designing a structure, we look at the grower's requirements, the site conditions, the crop's needs, the soil, drainage, the region and the local wind pattern. Each project is fitted to what the site requires, so the client gets the best solution for them.

Questions about net houses

What is an agricultural net house?

A structure of galvanized-steel poles and steel cables with a net over the crop. It protects orchards and crops from hail, sun, wind and other weather damage, saves water by reducing evaporation, and raises the quality and quantity of the crop grown under it.

Which net is used over bananas?

An 11% shade net: light and airy, over wide spans, with trellising and support built in.

Which net protects apples and cherries from hail?

A 10–30% shade net, mainly against hail and sunburn at flowering and fruit set. The structure can let the net fold away in winter and spread again in spring, by hand or automatically.

Who makes the poles, cables and nets?

Raw materials are ordered from the manufacturers to a production plan, then worked and fitted to the site in our plant. Poles, nets, cables and connecting fittings are packed and pass quality control before they are sent to the site.

What happens before a quote?

We receive a topographic map and the grower's requirements, check the data and make a preliminary design to build the client's quote.

Are there other shade solutions?

Yes, we also provide shade for agricultural work areas and for loading and unloading produce: a solution that suits the movement of forklifts, trucks, tractors and carts.

What about windbreaks and crop support systems?

We also offer windbreaks without a full net cover over the whole orchard, and a range of trellising and crop support systems.

Is there a grant for a net house in Israel?

The Investment Administration of the Ministry of Agriculture recognizes net houses for orchards and bananas in its development plan, in certain tracks and on certain conditions. Tracks and conditions change from year to year, so check the current procedure on the Ministry of Agriculture's website before you apply.
