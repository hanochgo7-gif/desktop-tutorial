Hail nets for orchards

A 10–30% shade net over the orchard, mainly against hail and sunburn at flowering and fruit set. The structure lets the net fold away in winter and spread again in summer.

Galvanized poles from our plant, set out on site to the design. MIFTAH archive.

The net house, in short

Net
10% to 30% shade, chosen for the crop
Protects
Mainly against hail and sunburn at flowering and fruit set
Season
The net folds away in winter and spreads in summer, by hand or with an automatic system
Trellising
Support for the crop, such as a pergola trellis for kiwi
Crops
Apple, cherry, kiwi, avocado, persimmon
Scope
We design and build the whole structure, from 5 ha (50 dunam)

How a project runs

  1. 01

    Site visit and needs assessment

    We meet you on the land and map the crop, soil, drainage, access and wind.

  2. 02

    Engineering design

    We calculate the loads and prepare the drawings and the bill of quantities: the list of materials the quote is based on.

  3. 03

    Manufacturing in our plant

    After the quote is approved, poles, nets, cables and fittings are made and checked in our plant.

  4. 04

    Installation and maintenance

    Our crews, trained for work at height, build the structure and maintain it over the years.

Questions

Which net protects apples and cherries from hail?

A 10–30% shade net, mainly against hail and sunburn at flowering and fruit set.

Can the net fold away in winter?

Yes. The structure is built so the net folds in winter and spreads in summer, by hand or automatically.

Can an orchard net house get a grant?

In the Ministry of Agriculture's 2023 development plan, net houses for apples, lychee, figs and kiwi are listed among the supported items, in certain tracks and on certain conditions. Check the current procedure before you apply.

Do you build kiwi pergolas?

Yes. The pergola trellis is built together with the net house.
