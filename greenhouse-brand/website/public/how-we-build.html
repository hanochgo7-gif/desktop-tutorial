How a net house is built

Scroll down and watch one go up: from bare soil, through galvanized poles, cables and net, to a protected orchard.

Illustration
01 / 10
Scroll to build
01 Design

Designed for the site

We map the crop, soil, drainage, access and wind, then design the structure and the bill of quantities.

02 On site

Marking out

Pole lines are marked from the engineering design, point by point.

03 From our plant

Galvanized poles

Poles made and checked in our plant are set in straight rows, at the span and height in the design.

04 At height

Cables and anchoring

Steel cables are tensioned across the pole heads, and the edges are anchored against the wind. Our crews are certified for work at height.

05 The net

Spread row by row

The net is unrolled over the cables and fixed, one bay at a time.

06 Closing

Side nets and a door

Side nets and an entrance door close the structure.

07 Planting

Irrigation, then trees

Drip lines go in first. Then the orchard is planted under the net.

08 What the net does

Hail stops on the net

The net catches hail before it reaches the fruit, and breaks the wind.

09 Winter

Folded, then spread again

On orchards the net can fold away in winter and spread again in summer, by hand or automatically.

10 One team

From design to maintenance

Design, our own plant, installation and maintenance, in Israel and abroad.
