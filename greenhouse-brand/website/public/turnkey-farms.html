Turnkey farms abroad

For investors, agricultural companies and entrepreneurs who want a working farm on their land, without hiring a contractor, an agronomist and a site manager separately.

Our newest line of work

We deliver turnkey farms together with agricultural partners. The structures at the core of the farm come from what we have built for more than 20 years, in Israel and on three continents.

A net house in Portugal

We built an avocado net house for a client in Portugal who came to us by referral. The client is willing to give a reference.

Where

Portugal and other European countries, Africa and other markets. Minimum farm size: 30 ha.

11 stages, and what you receive at each

One path, from the soil to the harvest.

Assess

  1. 01Soil testing and crop fitSoil test results and a crop recommendation for your site.
  2. 02Business plan and budgetA business plan and budget for your farm.

Design

  1. 03Farm designStructures, irrigation and roads laid out as one farm.

Build

  1. 04Structures and net housesNet houses, sheds and windbreaks, made in our plant and built to the design.
  2. 05Irrigation systemsIrrigation installed and working before planting.
  3. 06Procurement and logisticsMaterials bought, shipped and on site when the schedule needs them.
  4. 07PlantingThe orchard or crop planted and established.

Operate

  1. 08Local teamLocal workers recruited, trained and managed on site.
  2. 09Growing and agronomyDay-to-day growing management, season after season.
  3. 10MaintenanceStructures and systems kept in working order.
  4. 11Harvest and marketingThe crop harvested and marketed.

What stays with you

  • Local licensing and permits, with your local advisers.
  • Ongoing reporting to owners and investors on budget and progress, with your own advisers.

The most common mistake

Investors trying to set up the farm by themselves to save money.
