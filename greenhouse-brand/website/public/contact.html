Before you get in touch

What size of project do you take on?

Projects start from 5 ha (50 dunam) in Israel and from 30 ha abroad.

How is the price set?

After we review the site. Every structure is designed for its land: the crop and the net it needs, the area, the soil, drainage, access and the wind on site all go into the design. The quote is based on that design and its list of materials and quantities.

Do you work outside Israel?

Yes. We have built net houses in Mexico, India, Honduras, Spain, Georgia and Portugal, and ran a complex project in Belarus. Abroad, we plan the work, train local crews and manage the build on site.

Who delivers a turnkey farm?

MIFTAH, together with agricultural partners. Local licensing and permits, and ongoing reporting on budget and progress, stay with you and your advisers.

Can I talk to one of your clients?

Yes. Clients in Israel and in Portugal are willing to give a reference. We put you in touch after a first conversation.
