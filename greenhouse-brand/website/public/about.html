A family company that builds for growers

We initiate, design and carry out agricultural projects, from the project's infrastructure to every kind of agricultural structure. More than 20 years, from Kfar Masaryk in the north of Israel.

The owners

Merav Eyal Hasfel

Co-CEO and owner

BA in business administration. Runs the company day to day, its finance, and marketing and sales. Your first contact for a new project.

Yiftach Hasfel

Co-CEO and owner

Practical engineer in construction, trained at the Technion. Leads needs mapping, engineering design, development, quality control, production supervision and execution. He ran a complex project in Belarus that kept to schedule with local manufacturing, shipping and local crews.

How we work

Our own plant makes the poles, nets, cables and fittings, and checks them before they ship. Our installation crews are trained for work at height and run several projects in parallel. The team brings together Israelis and agronomy students from abroad who come to study in Israel.
