## One sentence

**MIFTAH Projects designs, manufactures in its own plant, installs and maintains galvanized-steel net houses for orchards and bananas, in Israel and abroad. For investors abroad, it builds complete farms together with agricultural partners.**

## Voice (Q q34: entrepreneurial; family and personal)

- **Say what we do in plain verbs:** design, make, build, maintain.
- **Use the client's own words where we have them:** "a word is a word", "like family", "peace of mind and time".
- **No superlatives:** no "leading", "world-class" or "innovative".
- **No promises the client didn't make:** no yields, ROI, delivery times or guarantees.
- **Say what's not included,** because it builds trust (permits, owner reporting).
