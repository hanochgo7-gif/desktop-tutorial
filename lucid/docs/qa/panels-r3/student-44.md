## What worked for me (keep this)

- **The placement test did not make me feel stupid.** "אין ציון ואין ״נכשל״. זו רק נקודת פתיחה." and "לא בטוחים? ״לא יודע/ת״ עדיף על ניחוש" are exactly what an anxious adult needs. It took 12 questions, not the feared 30. The result is kind and honest: "הראית שליטה עד רמה A1, בשתי סדרות של שאלות.", per skill "A1 (2 שאלות ברמה הזו)" and "לא נבדק במבחן הזה" for speaking and writing (06, 07, 08).
- **Lesson parts that fit 10 minutes.** The intro tells me in advance: "השיעור כולו כ-20 דקות, ולכן התוכנית היומית שלך לוקחת היום חלק ממנו: עד תרגיל 6 מתוך 15". After exercise 6 a calm screen says "החלק של היום הושלם ... המקום שלכם נשמר", and "לעצור כאן" is the main button. I can go to sleep without guilt (12, 14-part-end).
- **Speaking without fear.** "לא יכול/ה לדבר כרגע" is always there (my kids are asleep), and "תרגול דיבור לא מקבל ציון." I mark myself, and "צריך עוד תרגול" is answered with "נרשם: צריך עוד תרגול", not a red X. This is the most important screen for me, so please do not lose it (15-ex-14-e14-speak-full).
- **Pronunciation tips in body terms, in Hebrew.** "we ו-work מתחילות ב-/w/: עגלו את השפתיים כמו ל'אוּ' ואז פתחו אותן. לא v." In the speaking lab, ship/sheep, live/leave, fill/feel and bit/beat each have a play button and a Hebrew meaning (ספינה / כבשה). "איזו מילה שמעתם?" trains my ear before my mouth (15-ex-14, 35-speaking-c1).
- **Short, specific feedback on mistakes.** "לא מוסיפים are לפני הפועל: We go." and "My sister and I זה כמו we, ולכן בלי s: we play." One line, in Hebrew, about the mistake I actually made (15-ex-04-e4-fb, 15-ex-06-e6-fb).
- **Fair checking.** My lowercase translation with no full stop, "we live in jerusalem and work in tel aviv", was accepted as "נכון". "evry" was "כמעט!", not wrong (15-ex-09, 15-ex-07).
- **Review is gentle.** New words come as multiple choice with "לא זוכר/ת". Feedback shows the word, its meaning and an example ("meet = לפגוש, להיפגש / We meet our friends on Saturdays."). The done screen explains why: "מילים שהתבלבלתם בהן יחזרו מוקדם יותר" (23-review-1, 23-review-3-fb, 24).
- **The tone respects me.** "ערב טוב, רינה", "יעד שבועי: 1 מתוך 5 ימים. יום חופש לא מוחק כלום.", "אין רצף שנשבר". There is no mascot, no streak and no guilt (09, 22).
- **Words and pictures is easy to understand on the first try.** "בוחרים תמונה ומילה (בכל סדר)." A matched picture shows its Hebrew word, and the results list "מילים לחזור עליהן" with "לשחק עם המילים האלה (2)" (41, 43, 44).
- **Honest about AI.** The Practice hub says "מורה AI ... דורש הגדרת AI", and Settings says "(משוב AI עוד לא זמין באתר הזה.)" (30, 10).
- **Text size is easy to find once you are in Settings.** It is the fourth control, and 115% applies everywhere. Dark mode is comfortable at night (10).

## What did not work

### F1. The lesson-end "כדאי לשים לב" box talks about things I never learned (major)
- **Route:** `#/lesson/a1-01`, end screen.
- **What I did:** I made three mistakes in lesson 1: "are go", "plays" for my sister and I, and "is work". The end screen's "כדאי לשים לב" box says "צורת הפועל: פעלים חריגים צריך לזכור: go – went – gone." and "התאמה בין נושא לפועל: The people are, not The people is." I have never seen "went" or "gone". As a false beginner, my first thought was "I missed something, I'm behind". These are generic notes for an error type, not my mistakes (16-lesson-end).
- **Expected:** a reminder of what I actually got wrong, in this lesson's terms.
- **Suggestion:** On the end screen, show the item's own explanation, or the learner's actual wrong answer with its fix ("We are go → We go", "My sister and I plays → play"). Never use the general error-type note there, just as R2-04 already did inside the exercise feedback.

### F2. Pronunciation symbols and English fragments come out scrambled inside Hebrew lines (major)
- **Route:** `#/lesson/a1-02` intro (objective 3), `#/lesson/a1-01` learn page and end screen.
- **What I saw:** The objective "אני יודע/ת להגות את הסיומת כ-/s/, /z/ או /ɪz/" displays as "s/,/-...כ" and "z/: works, lives,ɪ/ או //z", with the slashes and letters in the wrong places. I cannot read it, and it is about pronunciation, the thing I worry about most (52b-ipa-crop). In the learn page warning box, "They live, לא They lives." displays as ".They lives לא ,They live" (13-learn-crop2). In "מה לזכור" the "I" of "I work in a bank." sits alone at the end of the Hebrew line (16-lesson-end).
- **Suggestion:** Isolate every IPA token (`/…/`) and every English run inside Hebrew text as its own left-to-right unit (`<bdi dir="ltr">`), including the slashes and the trailing punctuation. Add a test string with "כ-/s/, /z/ או /ɪz/" to the bidi checks.

### F3. At A1, the Hebrew explanation looks secondary and the "short explanation" is about 8 screens long (major)

The English line under it is larger and in the body colour (19.55px, near-black or white). In exercises, "בחרו את המילה הנכונה." is small and blue, while "Choose the correct word." is large and bold. At A1 the Hebrew is the part I can actually read, yet the design tells me the English is the important part. The learn page of lesson 1 is about 7,000 CSS px tall at 115% (the full screenshot is 14,062 px at 2x), and its footer says "הסבר קצר, ועכשיו מתרגלים." (13-lesson-learn, 53-a1-02-learn-light, 07-placement-q1).
- **Suggestion:** At "full" Hebrew support, make the Hebrew the primary text: body colour, full size, first. Fold the English duplicate behind a small "English" toggle, or show it smaller and muted. Keep English for examples, which is where I need it. That alone would roughly halve the learn page.

### F4. One wrong tap in the matching exercise turns the whole first exercise red (major)
- **Route:** `#/lesson/a1-01`, exercise 1 of 15 ("התאימו כל פועל למשמעות שלו בעברית").
- **What I did:** I matched all six verbs correctly, but on the way my thumb paired "live" with the wrong Hebrew word once. The verdict was a red "✕ לא בדיוק · ניסיון שגוי אחד". The exercise counts as missed on the first try (half a point), and it is added to "לחזרה". This was the very first exercise of my very first lesson, and every pair on screen was green (15-ex-01-e1-fb).
- **Suggestion:** Treat matching as learning, not a test. If every pair was found with at most one extra try, show "הותאם, עם ניסיון נוסף אחד" in amber or neutral, and do not re-queue it. Keep red for a real failure.

### F5. The first grammar table shows English word order backwards and squeezes the Hebrew (major)

### F6. On nights with reviews due, a 10-minute plan leaves room for only one exercise of the lesson (major)
- **Route:** `#/today` two nights later, then `#/lesson/a1-02`.
- **What I saw:** Today says "חזרה מרווחת · 11 מילים · 5 דק׳" and then "Present simple: he, she, it ... השיעור כולו כ-22 דקות, אז היום עושים חלק: עד תרגיל 1 מתוך 16 ... 5 דק׳". ## My top 3 wishes

1. **Hebrew first at my level.** Explanations where the Hebrew is the main text and the English duplicate is one tap away. Learn pages short enough that 10 minutes goes to practice. A lesson part never smaller than a few exercises, even on review nights.
2. **Pronunciation without fear, all the way through.** Keep the no-score, self-marked speaking and "לא יכול/ה לדבר כרגע". Replace phonetic symbols with Hebrew approximations and mouth instructions. Put a play button on every pronunciation tip, and fix the scrambled `/s/ /z/ /ɪz/` line.
3. **Forgiving, specific feedback.** No red verdict for one mis-tap. "Almost" that shows exactly what differed. An end-of-lesson box about the mistakes I actually made, never "go – went – gone" in lesson 1.
