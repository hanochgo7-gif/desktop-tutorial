## Audience, levels, price, Hebrew support

### Duolingo

- **Audience:** Mass-market, free-first, casual daily learners of all ages (130M MAU Q1 2025; 10.9M paid subs Jun 2025)
- **Levels:** English A1-B2 (B2 added Apr 2026); no C1/C2 claimed
- **Price:** Free with ads and Energy (25 points, drains per exercise). Super on IL App Store (seen 2026-10-30, periods not printed): ₪33.90/₪39.90, probably monthly, and ₪249.90-₪364.90, probably annual or family. US: $9.99-$12.99 and $83.99-$119.99. Max reported at $29.99/mo or $167.99/yr and may be sunset. Super Lite is a test (~$35.99/yr, one observation).
- **Hebrew support:** None: no English-from-Hebrew course and no Hebrew UI. English is offered from about 43 base languages, including Arabic and Russian.
- **Verdict:** Unmatched habit engine and cheap AI conversation, but it stops at B2, has no composition writing or Hebrew L1, and its gamification and Energy friction are drawing backlash.

### Babbel

- **Audience:** Paying adults who want practical conversation; L1-tailored courses for 9 display languages; strongest up to intermediate level (vendor's own words)
- **Levels:** English A1-C1 in 5 levels (C1 is something to 'work towards'); no C2
- **Price:** Subscription only. US vendor page (seen 2026-10-30): $107.40/yr, $80.70/6 mo, lifetime $299.99. IL App Store: All Languages 12 months ₪369; single language ₪149.90 per 3 months or ₪39.90 per month. Babbel Live discontinued for consumers.
- **Hebrew support:** No Hebrew course or Hebrew UI found; the full base-language list could not be verified (Hebrew is not among the 9 app display languages).
- **Verdict:** The most course-like and L1-aware option with calm AI speaking (Babbel Speak), but C1 is nominal, writing is just typing in context, and there is no Hebrew.

### Busuu

- **Audience:** Self-directed learners who want a structured CEFR course plus social and peer feedback; 14 languages
- **Levels:** A1-B2 for all languages; C1 for English only; AI Conversations levelled A1-C1
- **Price:** Free, Premium and Premium Plus. IL App Store (seen 2026-10-30): monthly ₪23.90-₪63.50; annual ₪113.90-₪379.90; 12-month Premium Plus ₪539. US: $6.99-$23.49 monthly; 12-month Premium $70-$138.99; 12-month Premium Plus $139.99. Tier is not printed on every item.
- **Hebrew support:** None: 15 UI and translation languages (including Arabic and Russian), no Hebrew
- **Verdict:** The only one with human inline correction of free writing and AI chat levelled to C1, but corrections have no rubric, tasks are short, the best AI is in the top tier, and there is no Hebrew.

### Memrise

- **Audience:** Vocabulary-first learners who want authentic native-speaker listening; 149 languages run by a 12-person team (vendor claim)
- **Levels:** Not mapped to CEFR: word lists built around A2, sentence builder toward B1, plus a free English CEFR checker and exam prep (IELTS and others)
- **Price:** Freemium plus Pro. IL App Store (seen 2026-10-30): ₪36.90/mo, ₪63.90 per 3 months, ₪154.90/yr (Annual Pro ₪167.90), lifetime ₪679.90 (offers ₪200-₪299.90). US: $24.99/mo, $61.99/yr, lifetime $329.99.
- **Hebrew support:** None: no official materials for Hebrew speakers and no Hebrew UI (22 UI languages, including Arabic, Persian and Russian)
- **Verdict:** The best authentic video input, plus a new AI-marked exam prep, but grammar and CEFR structure are thin, AI marking is unvalidated, and trust is dented by the 2024-25 migrations.

### ELSA Speak

- **Audience:** Professionals, job-seekers, students/test-takers, travelers; B2B (50+) and K-12 school plans; recent releases pivot to workplace coaches (sales, negotiation, leadership)
- **Levels:** A1-C2 claimed (auto-computed Profile level from pronunciation/fluency/listening/grammar); self-selected Learning Path rises 'up to C'
- **Price:** Free (2 pronunciation lessons/day, 3 AI lessons total). Tiers Pro / Premium / Professional. US App Store 2026-10-30: $19.99/mo, Premium $99.99/yr (other 1-yr SKUs $71-$129.99). Israel App Store 2026-10-30: Premium ₪264.90/yr, ₪89.90/3 mo; Pro ₪47.90/3 mo
- **Hebrew support:** None. 14 display and 12 mother-tongue/bilingual-mode languages exclude Hebrew; IL store lists English only; sold in Israel in ILS
- **Verdict:** Best-in-group phoneme-level pronunciation and IELTS-style speech analytics. No writing, no Hebrew, no placement test, and billing complaints dominate reviews.

### Speak

- **Audience:** Non-English speakers (15 L1s) who 'freeze' when speaking, especially beginners; also English speakers learning 6 languages; Speak for Business; age 13+
- **Levels:** Not stated (levels exist but no CEFR mapping; no advanced track: advanced learners pointed to Free Talk/Tutor)
- **Price:** 7-day trial, no free plan. US App Store 2026-10-30: Premium $17.99/mo or $83.99/yr; Premium Plus $39.99/mo or $164.99/yr. Israel App Store 2026-10-30: Premium ₪69.90-₪79.90/mo or ₪179.90/yr; Premium Plus ₪199.90/mo or ₪349.90/yr
- **Hebrew support:** None. 15 supported native languages and 16 app languages exclude Hebrew; sold in Israel in ILS (only 48 IL ratings)
- **Verdict:** Polished, human-reviewed beginner speaking curriculum on OpenAI speech tech. Lenient scoring, American English only, no writing, thin advanced content, and personalization locked in Premium Plus.

### Praktika

- **Audience:** Broad: teens, adults and seniors; conversation-first learners of 14 languages; B2B plan
- **Levels:** Not stated (help center mentions 'A0 to A2' beginner bands; claims 'first word to fluent conversation'; refers CEFR questions to support)
- **Price:** Free plan (3 avatars, limited practice). US App Store 2026-10-30: $9.99/mo, $49.99/3 mo, $119.99-$139.99/yr (promos $6.99-$49.99). Israel App Store 2026-10-30: ₪34.90/mo, ₪179.90/3 mo, ₪299.90/yr (other annual SKUs ₪149.90 and ₪399.90). Vendor: 50% off first plan via web
- **Hebrew support:** Not documented. Hebrew absent from 14 study languages and listed app languages (Arabic present); native-language toggle's L1 list unpublished; LLM Hebrew explanations not verified. Largest IL rating base (5.7K) of the four
- **Verdict:** Cheapest, most embodied AI-avatar conversation practice, with a slang tutor and native-language toggle. No CEFR structure, no writing, memory and recognition complaints, and advanced learners plateau.

### Rosetta Stone

- **Audience:** Individuals, enterprise and schools; 25 languages incl. English (US/UK) and Hebrew as a target; owned by IXL Learning; new consumer app 'Sapphire' launched 2026-08-04
- **Levels:** Enterprise Foundations A1-A2 (schools page claims Pre-A1-B2); enterprise Fluency Builder B1-C1; consumer Sapphire not mapped (LingQ: ceiling about A2)
- **Price:** Web 2026-10-30: $19.99/mo or $159/yr (all 25 languages), 7-day Sapphire trial. US App Store (Classic): English 3 mo $44.99; Unlimited 1 yr $109.99. Israel App Store (Classic, restricted to existing subscribers): English 3 mo ₪157.90/₪204.90, 12 mo ₪384.90, Lifetime ₪599.90
- **Hebrew support:** No Hebrew interface (11 web / 17 mobile UI languages, none Hebrew); immersion means no L1 explanations for anyone; teaches Hebrew as a target language; sold in Israel in ILS
- **Verdict:** Only competitor with a CEFR-labelled scope and sequence (to C1, enterprise), a WCAG 2.2 VPAT and live human tutors. But no grammar explanations, about A2 consumer ceiling, dictation-level writing, and lifetime-licence backlash.

### LingQ

- **Audience:** Self-directed learners of 50+ languages at roughly lower-intermediate level and above who want authentic reading and listening input; app rated 13+
- **Levels:** No CEFR mapping; six internal levels from Beginner 1 to Advanced 2 (per reviews)
- **Price:** lingq.com (USD, seen 2026-10-30): Free plan allows 20 saved LingQs and 5 imports. Premium $14.99/mo, $119.99/yr or $215.76/24 mo. Premium Plus $29.99/mo or $269.99/yr. Israel App Store (ILS): Premium ₪54.00/mo or ₪429.90/yr; Premium Plus ₪107.90/mo or ₪969.90/yr
- **Hebrew support:** Hebrew is a target language only. No Hebrew UI on web (18 UI languages) or iOS, and no English course taught from Hebrew. Dictionary language can be changed, but Hebrew gloss quality is not verified
- **Verdict:** Best-in-group engine for receptive B2-C2 input from authentic content, but no zero start, no Hebrew support and only peer, points-based writing correction.

### Quizlet

- **Audience:** School and college students and teachers studying any subject; not a language course (Common Sense Media: 11+)
- **Levels:** None (user-generated sets, no curriculum)
- **Price:** Official page blocked (HTTP 403). Third-party US web prices (Oct 2026): Plus $7.99/mo or $35.99/yr; Plus Unlimited $44.99/yr. US App Store in-app purchases: $9.99 and $44.99. Israel App Store: Plus ₪29.90 and ₪129.90 (billing periods not shown). Free tier has ads and daily Learn/Test caps
- **Hebrew support:** No Hebrew UI (17 UI languages, not Hebrew) and no English course. Users can make their own Hebrew-English sets. Hebrew text-to-speech and its tier are not verified
- **Verdict:** Excellent low-friction vocabulary drilling with research support, but a tool rather than a course, with no writing or speaking pedagogy, a retired AI tutor (Q-Chat) and heavy paywall backlash (Trustpilot 1.4/5).

### Cambridge Write & Improve

- **Audience:** EFL learners practising writing without a teacher, plus teachers (Class View) and IELTS/B2 First candidates (Test Zone)
- **Levels:** A1-C2 scoring; more than 250 free tasks; the 2018 model is least accurate at A1 and C2
- **Price:** Core tool free. +Test Zone £4.50/mo per workbook (GBP incl. VAT, seen 2026-10-30). +Class View £11/mo for 10 members, then £1.10-£0.80 per member (help article dated 2022). A July 2026 directory reports list prices withdrawn; this conflicts with the official pages
- **Hebrew support:** None. UI in English and Spanish only; feedback in English
- **Verdict:** Most credible free automated writing scorer (CEFR-validated, high-precision flags), but it assesses rather than teaches and gives no explanations or argument/coherence feedback, and it is weakest exactly at C2.

### British Council LearnEnglish

- **Audience:** Adult self-study learners (free LearnEnglish accounts are 18+), paying English Online students (the British Council English app is for registered students only), and job or university applicants (EnglishScore)
- **Levels:** LearnEnglish skills A1-C1 (speaking A1-B2; C2 listed but no C2 skills lessons). Grammar app Beginner (A1) to Advanced (C2) (vendor claim). EnglishScore 100-599 = A1-C1
- **Price:** LearnEnglish free (paid subscriptions closed to new subscribers). Grammar app free; Remove Ads US$1.99 / ₪4.90. English Online Silver/Gold/Platinum billed monthly in local currency (prices not shown on the official page on 2026-10-30; an undated third-party review lists US$88/$143/$177). British Council English app self-study US$22/3 mo or $69/12 mo. EnglishScore test free; certificate from US$24.99 (July 2026 guide)
- **Hebrew support:** None found. All products English-only; Grammar app UI in 5 other languages, not Hebrew. The British Council Israel site was unavailable (HTTP 503), so any local offering is not verified
- **Verdict:** Trusted, carefully edited C1 model texts and a mobile certificate test, but the free route gives no writing feedback and no progress tracking, stops at C1, and has no Hebrew support; AI feedback is limited to speaking in expensive paid tiers.

### Cambly

- **Audience:** Adults who want to improve speaking and listening, including professionals; Small Groups ages 21+; separate Kids (4-15) and Business products
- **Levels:** Adults: 'All levels welcome', no CEFR claim; Kids: A1-C1
- **Price:** Official page (USD, seen 2026-10-30, 50% promo shown): Small Groups from $24/mo (promo $12), Private+ from $56 ($28), Pro from $80 ($40). LearnEnglish.life (USD, 2026-10-30, 30 min/week): $39 / $82 / $103 per month billed monthly; $19.50 / $41 / $51.50 per month billed yearly; $1 trial (another review says no trial). ILS not verified.
- **Hebrew support:** None: interface in 12 languages without Hebrew; chat auto-translation and search for tutors who speak your language exist, but Hebrew support in either is not verified
- **Verdict:** On-demand conversation with native speakers but no curriculum, no writing track and no Hebrew; weak for true beginners and for C1-C2 writing.

### Preply

- **Audience:** Learners of 90+ languages; English is the largest category (46,957 English tutors); adults, business, test prep
- **Levels:** No platform CEFR claim; beginner/intermediate/advanced labels; tutor-dependent (one profile lists A0-A2; mentions a 'C1 English course', IELTS, TOEFL)
- **Price:** Official (USD, 2026-10-30): English tutors $3-$50+ per lesson; average $26/hr native, $23/hr non-native. One tutor per subscription, billed every 28 days, minimum 4 lessons per cycle, unscheduled lessons expire. Third-party (2026-10-25): range $3-$100, paid trials, unpublished processing fee. ILS not verified.
- **Hebrew support:** App interface in 19 languages, Hebrew not among them (US and IL storefronts); a Hebrew-speaking tutor can be found via the 'Also speaks' filter (count not verified); no Hebrew-medium course
- **Verdict:** Best way to get an L1-matched human tutor, with English-only AI lesson summaries, but quality, curriculum and writing support depend entirely on the tutor.

### Loora

- **Audience:** Adults 'who know the basics' and want speaking confidence; professionals (about 70% professional use in 2023); B2B (vendor claims 150+ organizations)
- **Levels:** Claims A1-C2 (help center), but says complete beginners should start elsewhere; no syllabus
- **Price:** US App Store (USD, 2026-10-30): monthly $14.99-$19.99, 3 months $44.99, yearly $59.99-$119.99. IL App Store (ILS, 2026-10-30): monthly ₪49.90-₪69.90, 3 months ₪119.90-₪149.90, yearly ₪199.90-₪409.90. Free trial; auto-renew; no refunds for unused time.
- **Hebrew support:** App interface localized into Hebrew (App Store); native-language Translate and 'How to say in English' lifeline; Hebrew as the selectable native language not directly verified; no Hebrew-medium instruction or curriculum
- **Verdict:** Strong, cheap, always-on AI speaking coach from a Tel Aviv company, but no writing, no syllabus, and it explicitly turns away complete beginners.

### Grammarly

- **Audience:** Global workforce, students and multilingual writers (vendor claims 40M+ users); not a course
- **Levels:** None (not a course; no CEFR mapping)
- **Price:** Plans page (USD, 2026-10-30): Free $0; Pro $12 (billing period not stated); Enterprise custom. Third-party (2026-07-15): Pro $12/mo billed annually ($144/yr), $60/quarter, $30 monthly. ILS not verified.
- **Hebrew support:** None: Hebrew absent from writing-support and 19 translation languages; L1 fluency tailoring covers only French, German, Hindi, Mandarin and Spanish
- **Verdict:** Deepest feedback on learners' own writing, including a rubric-based AI Grader, but it fixes rather than teaches; research flags weakness on argumentation and over-reliance; no Hebrew.

### Jerusalem Post LiteTalk

- **Audience:** Israeli adults (beginner, conversational, advanced), business/tech/legal English, teens 13-18 (Bagrut), kids 6-12, AMIRAM prep; vendor claims 10,000+ students in the past year and 200+ native English-speaking teachers
- **Levels:** CEFR-based placement check and AI coach claimed; range not stated; advanced course aims at 'near-native' without a C1/C2 claim
- **Price:** No public prices; free ~30-minute assessment lesson and free no-commitment trial; ILS pricing not verified
- **Hebrew support:** Hebrew website, sales and support; the AI coach speaks Hebrew and English and explains in Hebrew (vendor claim); human teachers are native English speakers whose Hebrew ability is not stated
- **Verdict:** The most Israel-specific option: phone/Zoom native-speaker coaching plus a new Hebrew-capable AI coach, but speaking-first, opaque on price, and with no academic or argumentative writing.
