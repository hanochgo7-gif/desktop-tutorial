# Team A — Competitor profiles: Core gamified / mass-market apps (Duolingo, Babbel, Busuu, Memrise)

> **Method.** Desk research done on 2026-10-30. Every claim below cites a page that was opened in this session with WebFetch. Search-result snippets were used only to find pages. Anything known only from a snippet was dropped or marked "not verified". The sources are official product and blog pages, Apple App Store listings for the Israeli (IL) and US storefronts, one public Duolingo course-catalogue endpoint, press coverage, peer-reviewed or academic work, and a few independent reviews. Several of those reviews are affiliate-funded or written by competitors, and they are labelled as such. Vendor marketing is labelled **(vendor claim)**. Unless noted otherwise, prices were read from App Store listings on 2026-10-30.
>
> **Limitations.** No app was tested hands-on: no accounts were made and no lessons were completed. These sites could not be read:
> - Blocked or 403: the Babbel and Busuu Zendesk help centres, Duolingo investor PDFs, PCMag, NYT Wirecutter, and Reddit (excluded by rule).
> - Rendered by JavaScript with no readable text: Duolingo's help centre and its course pages.
>
> On App Store listings, in-app purchase (IAP) prices often appear **without the billing period**. Where the period is not printed, this report says so instead of guessing. Pricing covers only the IL and US storefronts. Accessibility and offline behaviour come from documentation, not from device testing.

## Duolingo

DAUs grew 23% YoY in Q2 2026, and management's goal is 100M DAU in 2028 [9]. The App Store age rating is 4+ [2]. The product is built for casual, daily, short-session learners of every age, not specifically for adults chasing academic goals. | Official, press, app-store |
| 2 | Learning methodology | Short, gamified exercise sessions. New English B1–B2 content is "fully monolingual": learners infer meaning from context, with hints in their own language [5]. Since 2024–2025 the company has leaned on generative AI for content. Duolingo cut about 10% of contractors in Jan 2024, and an April 2025 "AI-first" memo told staff to accept "occasional small hits on quality". The same month it launched 148 AI-developed courses [6]. | Official, press (Wikipedia) |

Video Call with Falstaff for beginners (Jan 2026) gives "real-time feedback in your own language". It is Max-only, iOS-only, and covers nine languages including English [8]. On 2026-08-05 management said "most new Super subscribers get video calls" and existing subscribers would follow "over the next few months" [9]. Speaking outcomes (Spanish and French only, Versant test, vendor study): 66% and 53% reached A2 or higher, and French pronunciation was the weakest subscore [11]. | Official, press (earnings call) |
| 8 | AI integration (what, which tier) | Max was launched in March 2023 with GPT-4 for Roleplay and tailored grammar explanations [21][7]. Features: Explain My Answer (now free [6]), Roleplay (Max), and Video Call (Max, moving into Super [9]). Management: "we may actually sunset Max", and the cost is "now under $0.01 per video call", mainly because of a "move towards open source models" [9]. Generative AI also writes course content [6]. | Official, press |
| 9 | Personalization / adaptivity | Birdbrain personalises exercise difficulty for each learner. Vendor A/B claims say learners "learn more" and return more often. By 2020 it personalised more than 20% of lessons, and the post gives no effect sizes (vendor claim) [14]. Video Call remembers earlier calls [7]. No adaptation to the learner's goals, L1-specific error patterns, or writing level was found. | Official |
| 10 | UI & usability | App Store rating is 4.7 from 5.5M ratings (US) [3] and 4.7 from 60k (IL) [2], so the core UX is widely liked. There is no Hebrew or RTL UI [2]. Free-tier friction is a common complaint. PCWorld (May 2026) says the free version "feels set up to annoy you enough to subscribe" [16]. Under Energy, an Android Authority reviewer ran out during her third lesson [15]. | App-store, press, independent review |
| 11 | Subscription model & pricing | Freemium: ads plus Energy on free; Super; Max; a "Super Lite" test. **IL App Store (₪, seen 2026-10-30), periods not printed:** Super is listed at ₪33.90 and ₪39.90 (probably monthly) and at ₪249.90, ₪289.90 and ₪364.90 (probably annual or family). Gems cost ₪14.90 [2]. **US App Store (USD), periods not printed:** Super is listed at $9.99, $12.99, $83.99, $95.99 and $119.99 [3]. Max was reported at $29.99/month or $167.99/year earlier in 2026, and was not in Apple's US list in September 2026 [20]. Super Lite is a test tier. The CEO says it gives "twice as much energy" [9]; one reviewer saw $35.99/year [20]. | App-store, press, independent price roundup |
| 12 | Strengths & weaknesses | **Strengths:** the habit engine, scale, an adaptive exercise engine, a polished UX, AI conversation getting cheaper and moving into the main tier, and some peer-reviewed reading and listening evidence at A2 [13]. **Weaknesses:** stops at B2 [4]; no composition writing [12]; no Hebrew L1 [1]; quality concerns from the AI-first shift [6]; free-tier friction from Energy [15][16]; and efficacy evidence that is mostly vendor-authored and covers reading and listening only [11][12][13]. | Mixed |
| 13 | Retention mechanics & how they feel | Streaks, Streak Freezes, milestone animations, and Super's "Monthly Streak Repair" [2][18]. Duolingo says 7-day-streak learners are "3.6 times more likely to complete their course", and admits that later motivation shifts to "loss aversion" (vendor claim) [18]. Energy replaced Hearts: free users get 25 points, and energy "depletes with every exercise", even on perfect lessons. In an Android Authority poll of 17,983 readers, 62% disliked it [15]. A peer-reviewed L@S '22 study of 9 years of forum posts plus 15 interviews found "gamification misuse" hurts learning, well-being and ethics [17]. A PCWorld columnist "became more concerned about maintaining my Duolingo streak and gaining XP" than about learning [16]. **Feel:** compulsive and anxiety-driven for some users, and monetised through that friction. | Official, press, academic |

It praised the clear audio cues [19]. Dyslexia support and captioning: **not verified**. | App-store, independent review |
| 15 | Offline | **Not verified.** Third-party sources disagree (from "a few lessons in the current unit" to "up to 100 lessons"), and no official help page could be opened. The App Store descriptions do not mention offline use [2][3]. | n/a |
| 16 | English taught FROM Hebrew | **No.** There is no English course from Hebrew and no Hebrew UI [1][2][3]. A Hebrew speaker must use the English course from English or from another language they know. English is available from Arabic and Russian [1], which matters for Israeli Arabic and Russian speakers but not for Hebrew-dominant learners. | Official, app-store |

**What they do better than anyone.** Duolingo is the best at building a daily habit at huge scale. Its adaptive exercise selection [14] and very cheap AI conversation [9] keep beginners coming back. It also has the most published efficacy evidence of the four, though that evidence is vendor-affiliated and limited to receptive skills [13].

**Where they fall short for a Hebrew-speaking adult aiming at C1–C2 writing.**
- No Hebrew L1 and no RTL UI [1][2].
- The course stops at B2 [4].
- Writing is translation and typing, with no essays and no rubric feedback. Duolingo itself has not measured writing outcomes [12].
- Monolingual B1+ content gives Hebrew speakers no L1 scaffolding at the exact stage where grammar gets hard [5].
- The gamification and Energy friction suits casual learners more than a 16+ learner working toward an exam [15][17].

- No Hebrew L1 [26].

- With Babbel Live gone for consumers [23], no human feedback channel is left.

**What they do better than anyone.**
- Busuu is the only one of the four where a human reads and corrects a learner's free writing, with inline edits [34].
- It is the only one with AI conversation practice levelled up to C1 for English [36].
- Its CEFR ladder is the clearest of the four [32].

**Where they fall short for a Hebrew-speaking adult aiming at C1–C2 writing.**
- No Hebrew L1 [39].
- C1 is English-only and has no published depth, and there is no C2 [32].
- Writing feedback depends on unvetted volunteers with no rubric, on short tasks, with no dialogue [41]. That is the opposite of C1–C2 argumentative-essay coaching (thesis, cohesion, counter-argument, register).
- AI feedback is attached to speaking conversations, not essays, and sits behind the top tier [36][37].

## Cross-competitor observations

1. **None of the four teaches English from Hebrew.** Hebrew is not a UI or explanation language in any of them [1][2][26][39][46]. This is not hypothetical: an Israeli academic project (EngliFun) was started because Hebrew speakers find it hard to learn English through existing apps when "the instructions and explanations are usually not in Hebrew" [22]. Arabic- and Russian-speaking Israelis do have L1 options in Duolingo, Busuu and Memrise [1][39][46], so the gap is specific to Hebrew-dominant learners.
2. **The ceiling is B2, or a soft C1.** Duolingo stops at B2 [4]. Babbel says learners can "work towards" C1 and leaves C2 to practice outside the app [28]. Busuu offers C1 for English only [32]. Memrise does not map to CEFR at all [50]. **None claims C2**, and none offers argumentative or academic essay instruction.
3. **Writing feedback is the weakest dimension across all four.**
   - Duolingo has no composition tasks and has not measured writing outcomes [12].
   - Babbel's writing is typing in context [29].
   - Busuu's human corrections have no rubric and their reliability is questionable [41].
   - Memrise's AI examiner is new and unverified [52], and IDP warns against LLM essay grading [55].

   No product gives rubric-based, discourse-level feedback (thesis, cohesion, argument structure, register) on extended writing.
4. **AI conversation has become a commodity.**
   - Duolingo runs Video Call for under $0.01 per call, is moving it from Max into Super, and may sunset Max [9].
   - Babbel includes Speak "at no additional cost" [25].
   - Busuu and Memrise both ship AI chat [35][51].

   "Has an AI tutor" is no longer a differentiator. What would differentiate is the quality, calibration and pedagogy of the feedback.
5. **Gamification is diverging.** Duolingo is pushing harder (Energy, streak monetisation), and its users are pushing back: 62% of 17,983 poll respondents disliked Energy [15]. Peer-reviewed work documents "gamification misuse" [17]. Babbel positions itself as anti-gamification [29], and Memrise now talks of a motivation model that "celebrates effort" [53]. There is room for a premium, calm, goal-driven motivation model aimed at learners aged 16+.
6. **Nobody declares accessibility features.** All four App Store listings say the developer has not indicated any [2][26][39][46]. Busuu says it is not yet WCAG 2.1 AA conformant [44], and an independent blind-user test found unlabeled controls in Duolingo [19]. No competitor offers RTL Hebrew with screen-reader support.
7. **AI erodes offline use.** Memrise's CEO says live AI makes offline "impossible" for many features [48]. Babbel and Busuu offer downloadable lessons [26][38], and Duolingo's offline behaviour could not be verified.
8. **Efficacy evidence is thin where Lucid competes.** Published studies cover Spanish or French, or English reading and listening at A2. They are vendor-authored or vendor-affiliated [11][12][13][42], or they measure oral proficiency for Spanish only [30]. None measures English writing at B2 or above.
9. **Israeli price anchors (IL App Store, ₪, 2026-10-30).**

   | App | Monthly | Annual | Other |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Duolingo Super | ₪33.90–39.90 | ₪249.90–364.90 | periods not printed [2] |
   | Babbel | ₪39.90 (one language) | ₪369 (all languages) | — [26] |
   | Busuu | ₪23.90–63.50 | ₪113.90–379.90 | Premium Plus ₪539/yr [39] |
   | Memrise | ₪36.90 | ₪154.90 | lifetime ₪679.90 [46] |

   A premium Hebrew-first product would likely sit at or above Busuu Premium Plus. A proven writing-to-C1 outcome would have to justify that price.
