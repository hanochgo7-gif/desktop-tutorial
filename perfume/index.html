Four hours of Unforgettable

From lamplight to morning

01 / 04

  1. I

    Lamplight

    Brass warms first. A circle of amber on a dark desk, and everything outside it waits its turn.

  2. II

    Rain

    The city keeps its distance behind wet glass. Inside, the air is still enough to hold something.

  3. III

    Skin

    Once, at the collar. Not to be noticed across a room — to be remembered in it.

  4. IV

    Morning

    By daylight the chair is cold and the lamp is off. The room is still not empty.
