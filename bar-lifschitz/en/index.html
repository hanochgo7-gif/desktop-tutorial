The menu

It changes with the season.

  1. To begin
    • Focaccia, olives and bishbash
    • Pan-tossed brown butter, fresh hyssop and shallot jam
  2. Cold
    • Sliced cured pork neck, stracciatella, basil oil, pine nuts and seasonal fruit
    • Shaved cucumber salad, arugula, toasted almonds and sumac onion on smoked labneh
    • Rump steak tartare, with thyme aioli and confit garlic
  3. From the sea
    • Local fish sashimi, avocado, red onion consommé, chili and rocket
    • Fish tartare, green mango, mint and arugula, with yogurt and green oil
  4. Hot
    • Tortellini in corn cream, with a caramelized onion, butter and wine sauce and flaked fish
    • Chorizo, milk bun, pepper cream, provençal and pickled onion
  5. Sweet
    • "Beirut Nights" style, za'atar crumble, raspberries and orange caramel syrup
    • Chocolate crémeux, olive oil and Maldon salt

Vegetarian, gluten-free, nut-free or lactose-free on request. Wine from local wineries, or your own. And at the end, a little something to take home.

Questions before you come

Can't find an answer? Message me on WhatsApp.

Length of the evening

Three hours, 6:30 to 9:30 pm.

Number of guests

In the yard 6 to 16. At your place 8 to 40.

Price

It depends on the menu, the number of guests and the place. Tell me, and I will send a proposal.

Allergies and preferences

Vegetarian, gluten-free, nut-free or lactose-free on request. The kitchen is not kosher.

Wine and drinks

Wine from local wineries, or your own. At events, cocktails by Kuller too.

Children

Dinners in the yard are for adults only.

Before you come

Arrive on time, with a warm layer for the evening. The address comes with the confirmation.

Accessibility

There are steps at the entrance. A wheelchair can reach the house from the road.

In winter

A studio in Matat opens soon. Until then, we plan around the forecast.

Deposit and cancellations

A 30% deposit holds the date, by PayBox or bank transfer. Cancel up to a month before: full refund. After that: no refund. In war or an emergency, cancel up to three days before: 50% back.
