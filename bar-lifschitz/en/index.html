My love for cooking came from my love of hosting, and from the connection it creates between people.

I grew up in an Argentinian home, where there were always guests and always something on the stove. After the army I started cooking in professional kitchens, and today I cook out of a love for hosting.

My cooking is simple, interesting and rooted in the Galilee. Every dish is made in front of the guests, with fresh, good ingredients and wine from local wineries.

Because a meal is so much more than food.

The menu

This is what a menu looks like with me, from the first bite to dessert. It changes with the season and the occasion.

  1. To begin
    • Focaccia, olives and bishbash
    • Pan-tossed brown butter, fresh hyssop and shallot jam
  2. Cold
    • Sliced cured pork neck, stracciatella, basil oil, pine nuts and seasonal fruit
    • Shaved cucumber salad, arugula, toasted almonds and sumac onion on smoked labneh
    • Rump steak tartare, with thyme aioli and confit garlic
  3. From the sea
    • Local fish sashimi, avocado, red onion consommé, chili and rocket
    • Fish tartare, green mango, mint and arugula, with yogurt and green oil
  4. Hot
    • Tortellini in corn cream, with a caramelized onion, butter and wine sauce and flaked fish
    • Chorizo, milk bun, pepper cream, provençal and pickled onion
  5. Sweet
    • "Beirut Nights" style, za'atar crumble, raspberries and orange caramel syrup
    • Chocolate crémeux, olive oil and Maldon salt

Can be adapted for vegetarians, gluten-free, nut allergies and lactose-free. Wine from local wineries, and you are welcome to bring your own.

Before you come

Can't find an answer? Message me on WhatsApp.

How long is a dinner in the yard?

Three hours, usually from 6:30 pm to 9:30 pm.

How many guests?

In the yard: 6 to 16. At your place: 8 to 40. A studio in Matat for events of 30 to 40 guests is opening soon.

How much does it cost?

Each dinner is priced by the menu, the number of guests and the place. Tell me what you have in mind and I will send a proposal.

Allergies and preferences

I adapt the menu for vegetarians, gluten-free, nut allergies and lactose-free. Let me know when you book. The kitchen is not kosher.

Wine and drinks

There is wine from local wineries, and you are welcome to bring your own. For events, cocktails by Kuller can be added.

Can we bring children?

Dinners in the yard are for adults only.

What should we know before we come?

Please arrive on time. Evenings in Matat are cool, so bring an extra layer. The exact address is sent with your booking confirmation.

Accessibility

There are steps at the entrance. A wheelchair can reach the house from the road.

What about winter?

A studio in Matat is opening soon, so rainy days will be covered too. Until then, we plan together around the forecast.

Deposit and cancellations

The date is held with a 30% deposit, by PayBox or bank transfer. Cancel up to a month before the dinner and the full deposit is returned; after that it is non-refundable. In case of war or an emergency, cancelling up to three days before the event returns 50% of the deposit.
