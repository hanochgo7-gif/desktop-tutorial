FAQ

Good questions to ask before you start.

What does the first consultation include?

A conversation with a professional who gets to know your business and what's on your mind. You leave with an initial direction: what to look into, what the right next step is and what can wait.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. The first call costs nothing and doesn't commit you to anything. Its purpose is to find out whether and how we can help.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. We work with small and mid-sized businesses, from one person up to 50 employees, as well as with individuals.

Is the service delivered remotely?

Yes. The service is remote, which is why we work with clients all over Israel.

Can I get ongoing support?

Yes. If you want a consistent professional on your side, you can continue with a monthly engagement. We define the scope together, based on what your business needs.

Do I have to commit to a monthly plan?

No. The first consultation commits you to nothing. A monthly engagement is an option for those who want it, and its terms are agreed in advance.

How do I get started with SOTA?

Fill in the form, message us on WhatsApp or book a call. We'll get back to you, set up an intro call and work out the next step together.

I'm already a client. How do I open a support ticket?

On the support ticket page. Fill in a few details, attach a screenshot if you have one, and you'll get a ticket number to track it.
