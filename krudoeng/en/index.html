How it works

  1. 1

    First, the inspection.

    Usually two to three hours for an apartment, without opening anything up: a laser level, spirit levels, a moisture meter and a thermal camera, against the plans, the specification and the Israeli standards.

  2. 2

    Then, the report.

    A section for every finding: photos, the clause of the standard, a repair recommendation and a cost estimate from the Dekel price list. A PDF by WhatsApp and email, within a week.

  3. 3

    Then we go through it together.

    Ravid explains what each finding means and what to do about it.

  4. 4

    And he stays available.

    For questions, advice and support with the developer or seller. A follow-up inspection after repairs can be arranged.

Ravid Krudo

Ravid is a registered construction practical engineer (registration 38633308), a graduate of the civil engineering faculty at ORT College, and a certified building defects inspector of the Israel Association of Construction and Infrastructure Engineers.

He comes to inspections from construction itself: he managed and supervised a self-build project of about 80 houses, from the permit to the finishes, managed the fit-out of offices of up to about 500 m² and of food businesses, and took projects through building permits and occupancy (Form 4).
