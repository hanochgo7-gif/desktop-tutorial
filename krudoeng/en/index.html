Which inspection do you need?

  • Second-hand apartmentBefore you signThe real condition of the property, with repair-cost estimates for the negotiation
  • Private house or villaBefore buying, or when something is wrongThe whole structure and its systems in one inspection
  • New apartment from a developerAround the handover protocolAn organised list of defects to hand to the developer
  • Common areasFor the house committee, at building handoverOne report on the shared property
  • After renovationWhen the works are finishedDefects found in time, while the renovation contractor is still responsible
  • Engineering expert opinionCracks, recurring damp, failures, disputesThe cause, extent and repair, in a reasoned document
  • Counter-inspectionWhen a report or opinion already existsAn independent review of the property and of what was written

  1. First, the inspection.

    Usually two to three hours for an apartment, without opening anything up: a laser level, spirit levels, a moisture meter and a thermal camera, against the plans, the specification and the Israeli standards.

  2. Then, the report.

    A section for every finding: photos, the clause of the standard, a repair recommendation and a cost estimate from the Dekel price list. A PDF by WhatsApp and email, within a week.

  3. Then we go through it together.

    Ravid explains what each finding means and what to do about it.

  4. And he stays available.

    For questions, advice and support with the developer or seller. A follow-up inspection after repairs can be arranged.
