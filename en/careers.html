About the role

Since 1972, S. Gotovski Ltd. has built the infrastructure beneath the fuel station forecourt, and from that same experience, the fuel infrastructure that feeds emergency generators at data centers. We are looking for a civil engineer or practical engineer for a meaningful, varied role with broad responsibility, combining engineering, procurement, planning and control.

This is your chance to join a dynamic company, take part in complex engineering projects, shape processes end to end and grow with us into more senior roles.

What the role involves

  • Managing engineering interfaces with consultants, designers, supervisors and clients
  • Coordinating approvals of materials, equipment and engineering specifications
  • Managing engineering procurement, orders and delivery tracking
  • Reviewing drawings, quantity take-offs and construction control
  • Checking contractor and supplier invoices, and budget control
  • Building work processes, reports, and tracking and control systems

Requirements

Must have

  • Registered civil engineer or practical engineer
  • At least 3 years of experience in a similar role on infrastructure, civil engineering or industrial projects
  • Excellent command of Excel

An advantage

  • Reading AutoCAD drawings and familiarity with Binaria software
  • Experience in fuel, gas or energy infrastructure (a significant advantage)
  • Independence, initiative, systems thinking, the ability to drive interfaces, and attention to detail

Why join us

  • Excellent, rewarding pay, according to experience and skills
  • Company car
  • Real influence and responsibility on significant projects
  • Professional and management advancement in a growing company
  • A dynamic, professional and entrepreneurial work environment
