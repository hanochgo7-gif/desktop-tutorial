From fuel station to data center

A data center is backed up by emergency generators, and the generators depend on the fuel infrastructure: large storage tanks, day tanks beside each generator, and feed lines that must keep working even when one line is out of service. It is the same engineering as a fuel station, on a larger scale and with a reliability requirement that leaves no room for error.

Decades of experience with fuel stations, double-wall tanks, sealed piping and documented testing, led us to this field. We work in coordination with the electrical consultants, the designers and the regulators, and hand over infrastructure tested section by section.

What the service includes

What the fuel infrastructure includes

  • Pits and foundationsExcavation, containment liners, anchoring and concrete foundations for tanks and equipment.
  • Underground tanksDouble-wall storage tanks, lifting, placement and connection.
  • Above-ground tanks and day tanksAbove-ground tanks on steel frames and day tanks beside the generators.
  • N+1 feed linesRedundant feed lines so every unit keeps receiving fuel even when one line is out of service.
  • Transfer pumps and valvesTransfer systems, valve manifolds and fill systems.
  • Leak detection and monitoringSensors, tank monitoring and connection to the site's control systems.
  • Testing and documentationPressure and tightness testing of every section, with documentation for handover.
  • Professional coordinationWorking with the electrical consultants, the designers and the regulators throughout the project.

FAQ

Questions about Fuel Infrastructure for Data Centers

What is an N+1 configuration in feed lines?

Every unit receives fuel from at least two lines, so if one line is out of service, for maintenance or a fault, the generators keep receiving fuel.

Do you work with the site's electrical consultants?

Yes. The fuel infrastructure is designed and built in coordination with the electrical consultants, the designers and the regulators, and connected to the site's control systems.

What is the difference between a storage tank and a day tank?

The storage tank is the main reservoir, usually underground and double-wall. The day tank is smaller, sits beside the generator, and is fed from the storage tank through the feed lines.
