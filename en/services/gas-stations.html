Fields of Work

LPG stations, from the vessels to the dispenser

Construction and renovation of autogas (LPG) fueling stations: burying vessels, concrete slabs, LPG systems, fire protection and the dispenser island, in coordination with the LPG company and the certifying bodies.

Request a quote

An LPG station demands a different precision

Liquefied petroleum gas is stored under pressure, so an LPG station is built to its own safety rules: distances from electrical pits and trenches, anchoring the vessels to a concrete slab, fire suppression systems and suitable lighting. The work is done under close supervision of the LPG company's engineers and to the standards of the Ministry of Environmental Protection and the Ministry of Labor.

We carry out all the infrastructure: excavation and burial, concrete, electrical and communications, LPG systems, the dispenser island and roof, and hand over a station ready to operate.

How it works

LPG station in five stages

  1. 01Planning and coordination

    Coordination with the LPG company, checking safety distances and planning the sequence of work.

  2. 02Excavation and burial

    Excavation to the required depth, placing the vessels and pouring the concrete slab that anchors them.

  3. 03Infrastructure

    Trenches, electrical, communications and concrete slabs for the equipment.

  4. 04LPG and safety systems

    LPG systems, compressor, fire protection and lighting.

  5. 05Dispenser island and handover

    Building the dispenser island and roof, installing the dispenser, testing and handover.

FAQ

Before you build a LPG station

Who supervises the construction of an LPG station?

The LPG company's engineers supervise the work, and it is carried out to the standards of the Ministry of Environmental Protection and the Ministry of Labor. We coordinate with them throughout the project.

Can an LPG dispenser be added to an existing fuel station?

Yes, subject to the safety distances and approvals. We check feasibility on site before quoting.

What is the difference between burying an LPG vessel and burying a fuel tank?

The vessel stores gas under pressure, so it is anchored to a concrete slab, with safety distances, fire suppression systems and a compressor. The principles of precise excavation and sealed infrastructure are common to both.
