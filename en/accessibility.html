Accessibility Statement

S. Gotovski attaches great importance to providing equal service to the whole public, including people with disabilities. We invest resources in making the site accessible, in accordance with the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Regulations (Service Accessibility Adjustments), 2013, and Israeli Standard SI 5568, which is based on the WCAG 2.0 guidelines at level AA.

Accessibility features on the site

  • The site is optimized for common browsers and mobile devices.
  • The site can be navigated with the keyboard alone. The Tab key moves between elements and Enter activates them. A "Skip to content" link appears on the first press of Tab.
  • The semantic structure of the pages (headings, navigation regions, main content) is adapted for screen readers.
  • All meaningful images have alternative text.
  • The color contrast between text and background meets the standard's requirements.
  • The site respects the operating system's "reduce motion" setting.
  • The accessibility menu (the round button at the bottom of the screen) allows larger text, high contrast, highlighted links, a readable font, stopping animations, a large cursor and highlighted keyboard focus. Your preferences are saved in your browser.

Known limitations

The Google map embedded on the Contact page is provided by a third party and is not fully under our control. The full address also appears as text beside the map. Despite our efforts, some parts of the site may not yet be fully accessible. We continue to improve accessibility on an ongoing basis.
