Accessibility statement

Last updated: October 9, 2026

S. Gotovski Ltd. wants everyone to be able to use the site and receive its service, including people with disabilities. The site was made accessible under the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 5773-2013, and the Israeli Standard IS 5568, which is based on WCAG 2.0 level AA.

What we did on the site

  • The site works in common browsers, on desktop and on phones.
  • The whole site can be used with a keyboard alone: Tab moves between elements and Enter activates them. A "Skip to content" link appears on the first Tab press.
  • Pages have a clear structure of headings, navigation areas and main content, suitable for screen readers.
  • Images that carry information have a text alternative.
  • Text-to-background color contrast meets the standard.
  • Buttons and links are large enough to tap comfortably on a phone, and text is a readable size.
  • Every form field has a label, and error messages appear next to the field itself.
  • The site respects the operating system's "reduce motion" setting.
  • The PDF documents on the site are built as accessible documents, with defined structure and language.
  • Accessibility menu: the round button in the corner of the screen offers larger text, high contrast, highlighted links, a readable font, stopped animations, a large cursor and highlighted keyboard focus. Your preferences are saved in your browser only.

Accessibility was checked with an automated test of every page on the site, on desktop and on phone, and with manual checks.

Known limitations

  • The live simulation and the 360° tour on the data center page are visual elements. The information in them is also explained in the text on the same page.
  • The Google map on the contact page is provided by a third party and is not fully under our control. The full address also appears as text and as a navigation link.

Despite our efforts, some parts of the site may not yet be fully accessible. We would be glad to hear about them and fix them.

Service at the office

Most of our service is given by phone, WhatsApp, email and at the client's work site. If you want to come to the office in Moshav Bnaya and need an accessibility adjustment, please arrange it with us in advance and we will find a suitable solution together.
