Privacy Policy

S. Gotovski (the "Company") respects the privacy of the site's users and acts in accordance with the Protection of Privacy Law, 1981, and the regulations under it. This document explains what information is collected on the site, for what purpose, and what your rights are.

Information collected

Information you provide

In the contact form you will be asked for a name, phone number, email address (optional), project type and a short description. There is no legal obligation to provide this information, but without it we cannot get back to you. The information is used solely to respond to your inquiry and to arrange a call or meeting.

Information collected automatically

The site may use statistical measurement tools (such as Google Analytics) that collect anonymous information about how the site is used, such as pages viewed, visit duration and device type. This information does not identify you personally and is used only to improve the site. Cookies can be blocked in your browser settings.

Third-party services

  • WhatsApp: Clicking the WhatsApp button opens a conversation in the app, subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy.
  • Google Maps: The map on the Contact page is loaded from Google servers, subject to Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts: The site fonts are loaded from Google servers.

Data retention and security

Inquiries received are kept in the company's systems for the purpose of handling them and only for the period required. The company takes reasonable measures to secure the information, but cannot guarantee absolute immunity from unauthorized access.

Sharing information with third parties

The company will not pass your details to third parties, except where required by law, or to service providers acting on its behalf to handle your inquiry.

Mailings

The company will not send you advertising material without your explicit prior consent, in accordance with Section 30A of the Communications Law (Telecommunications and Broadcasting), 1982.

Your rights

Under the Protection of Privacy Law, you are entitled to review the information held about you and to request its correction or deletion. For inquiries: Office@gotovski.co.il or by phone at 08-622-9307.

Changes to this policy

The company may update this policy from time to time. The current version will be published on this page.
