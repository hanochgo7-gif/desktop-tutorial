FAQ

Before you pick up the phone

Can a station be renovated without closing it?

Yes. Most of our renovations take place at operating stations. We plan the work in phases, fence off each work zone, and keep some of the dispensers open throughout the project.

How long does it take to build a new fuel station?

It depends on the scope, the permits and the ground conditions. In the first call we get to know the project and give a clear schedule estimate, and the quote breaks it down by stage.

Which standards does the work meet?

The standards of the Ministry of Environmental Protection and the Ministry of Labor: Stage II vapor recovery, double-wall tanks and piping, 5-gallon spill containment, cathodic protection and static grounding, oil separators, and documented pressure and tightness testing.
